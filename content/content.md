## Solutions for Every Industry

Tailored compliance workflows designed to meet the specific challenges of your sector.

Cards show "Click to learn more" and expand into the detailed copy. CTA in the detail view: Explore [Industry] Solutions.

### Healthcare
Short: Achieve full HIPAA compliance and protect sensitive patient data with automated workflows.

In healthcare, compliance isn't just about passing audits—it's about patient safety and protecting highly sensitive personal health information (PHI).

SysComply centralizes your quality management (ISO 9001), information security (ISO 27001), and compliance protocols. By replacing scattered documents with automated, trackable workflows, organizations can eliminate critical errors caused by outdated procedures.

- Secure patient data through strict access controls
- Automate incident reporting and CAPA workflows
- Ensure all staff are trained on the latest protocols

### Financial Services
Short: Navigate complex regulations like SOC2, GLBA, and ISO 27001 with robust security measures.

Financial institutions face unprecedented regulatory scrutiny. Manual compliance leaves unacceptable blind spots that can lead to massive fines or loss of operating licenses.

Our platform provides real-time risk assessment and continuous control monitoring. It connects your IT infrastructure directly to your compliance frameworks (SOC2, ISO 27001), ensuring that your "vault" is constantly monitored and secure.

- Continuous monitoring of security controls
- Automated risk registers and impact assessments
- Streamlined reporting for regulatory bodies

### Technology (SaaS)
Short: Fast-track SOC2 and ISO 27001 certification to close enterprise deals faster.

For SaaS companies, compliance is a massive sales enabler. Without SOC2 or ISO 27001, enterprise deals stall in procurement. But achieving these certifications manually drains engineering resources.

SysComply automates evidence collection by integrating directly with your cloud infrastructure. We turn compliance from an engineering bottleneck into a continuous, automated process that accelerates your sales cycle.

- Automated evidence collection from AWS/GCP/Azure
- Pre-built policies for SOC2 and ISO 27001
- Instantly generate compliance reports for prospects

### Manufacturing
Short: Streamline ISO 9001 quality management and supply chain standards across sites.

Disconnected quality systems lead to defects, costly recalls, and supply chain disruptions. In modern manufacturing, continuous traceability is mandatory.

SysComply digitalizes your ISO 9001 (Quality) and ISO 14001 (Environmental) management systems. It provides a unified view across multiple production sites, ensuring standard operating procedures (SOPs) are strictly followed everywhere.

- Unbroken traceability from raw material to finished good
- Digitized SOPs available on the factory floor
- Centralized non-conformance tracking across all sites

### Food & Beverage
Short: Ensure food safety and quality with ISO 22000 and HACCP compliance.

Food safety incidents can destroy a brand overnight. Relying on paper logs for temperature checks and hazard tracking is a recipe for disaster.

We transform food safety from reactive panic to a proactive culture. SysComply manages your ISO 22000 and HACCP plans digitally, ensuring hazard thresholds are monitored and deviations are caught instantly.

- Reduce HACCP documentation time by over 60%
- Automated alerts when approaching critical limits
- Instant recall readiness and traceability

### Mining & Resources
Short: Adhere to strict HSE standards and environmental regulations across remote sites.

Operating in high-risk, remote environments means that health, safety, and environmental compliance are literal licenses to operate. Mistakes cost lives.

SysComply connects remote sites to the central hub. It enforces strict adherence to ISO 45001 (Health & Safety) standards, ensuring incidents are reported immediately and preventative actions are universally deployed.

- Offline-capable incident reporting for remote sites
- Real-time safety dashboards for executive oversight
- Automated environmental impact monitoring
